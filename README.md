# Supply-chain-and-Logistics-Management-System-Analysis

This project presents a complete supply chain and logistics analysis using Power BI dashboards.  
Each dashboard highlights a different business area and helps decision-makers monitor performance, identify issues, and improve results.

## 1) Supply Chain and Logistics Overview
![Supply Chain and Logistics Overview](./Supply%20Chain%20and%20Logistics_01.png)

**Short description:**  
This dashboard gives a high-level view of end-to-end supply chain performance.

**What we found:**  
- Overall trends across operations, movement of goods, and major performance indicators.
- Areas where performance is stable vs. where attention is needed.

**Results from dashboard:**  
- A single, centralized operational snapshot.
- Faster understanding of total supply chain health.

**How it helps business analyze data:**  
It helps leadership quickly review overall supply chain status and prioritize detailed analysis in weaker areas.

## 2) Track Sales Performance
![Track Sales Performance](./Track%20Sales%20Performance_02.png)

**Short description:**  
This dashboard focuses on sales behavior, trends, and contribution across products/regions/time.

**What we found:**  
- Patterns in sales performance and demand movement.
- Better- and lower-performing segments.

**Results from dashboard:**  
- Clear view of sales growth/decline trends.
- Improved identification of high-value sales segments.

**How it helps business analyze data:**  
It supports data-driven sales planning, demand forecasting, and inventory alignment with actual sales patterns.

## 3) Supplier Performance
![Supplier Performance](./Supplier%20Performance_03.png)

**Short description:**  
This dashboard evaluates supplier reliability and contribution to supply chain success.

**What we found:**

**Results from dashboard:**

**How it helps business analyze data:**

## Overall Business Impact
